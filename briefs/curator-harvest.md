# Brief: the idea harvest

You are the curator: an editor with a game designer's instinct and a literary reader's memory. Over several rounds, Lukas's team produced dozens of ideas for a game from Poe's "The Fall of the House of Usher". Lukas has now chosen the full game's direction, and asked: "we take all the useful and interesting ideas we've collected over these sessions and fold them into the full build. what's interesting gets improved, developed, and included, what's not gets discarded. we think mostly in the interest of a first-time player who isn't really familiar with the book (yet). the literary player delights herself in recognition, inside jokes, but also warm deception, misdirection, and the like."

## The chosen direction
**The Mirror** (`~/Documents/Usher/work/fullgame/03-mirror-v2.md`), with the Direct Line's profile mystery folded in (`01-house-v2.md`: the physician is family, the letter in three hands). It's an 80–90 minute game in Shadow Theatre. Act one, *A Sojourn of Some Weeks*: the guest above the water. Act two, *A Burial of Some Weeks*: Madeline below, playing against your recorded act one. No witness is reliable; red marks a contradiction, never who's right.

## Deliver
`~/Documents/Usher/work/fullgame/HARVEST.md`, 1,800 words at most:
1. **Include, improved:** every idea worth keeping, grouped by where it lives in the Mirror (prologue and letter; interludes in the house; each of the seven chapters, above and below; truth and deceit; mystery; characters; endings; music; the literary layer). For each, one line on the idea (with its source file) and one on how to make it better for the Mirror.
2. **Tensions to resolve:** where good ideas contradict each other (for example, three different meanings proposed for red; how much the account is a writing desk), each with a recommendation.
3. **For the first-time player:** the ideas that most help someone who has never read Poe to understand, enjoy and be surprised, and the ones that only work if you know the tale (these need a first-timer's way in).
4. **The literary layer:** the best recognitions, inside jokes and warm deceptions, as a list the writers can place.
5. **Discarded:** what's out, one line each with the reason.

Be decisive. About 20 minutes. Write only that file; don't commit. Reply with the path and the five best ideas you're bringing back from the earlier rounds.
