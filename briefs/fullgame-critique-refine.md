# Full-game blueprints: critique and refine (one round)

All three blueprints are in:
- `~/Documents/Usher/work/fullgame/01-house.md` (mystery designer: the house)
- `~/Documents/Usher/work/fullgame/02-account.md` (systems and dialogue designer: the account)
- `~/Documents/Usher/work/fullgame/03-mirror.md` (structure designer: the mirror)

## 1. Critique the other two
Read both in full. Write `~/Documents/Usher/work/fullgame/critique-<your slug>.md` (house / account / mirror), 300–500 words. For each: its **strongest idea** (worth stealing even if the rest goes); its **weakest point** as an evening someone finishes and talks about; whether its **deceit is delightful and fair** or arbitrary and predictable (Lukas: "it shouldn't always be clear what is true and the deceit should be delightful… reflections can be tricky"); and its **build risk**. Close with a two-sentence **Bet**: which of the three, yours included, you'd build, and why. Don't vote for yours by reflex.

## 2. Refine yours
Write `~/Documents/Usher/work/fullgame/<NN>-<slug>-v2.md`, leaving the original unchanged. Open with **What changed and why** (at most 120 words), then the same thirteen sections, at most 1,400 words in total. Fix what the others exposed and steal what's better (credit it). **Stay distinct:** if you're converging with another blueprint, push toward what only yours can do. Sharpen section 11 into a **first milestone** Lukas can play within a day that proves the new game's core.

About 20 minutes. Touch nothing else; don't commit. Reply with both paths, your Bet line, and a two-sentence summary of your blueprint as it now stands.
