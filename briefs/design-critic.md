# Brief: critic for the mini-games of "A Sojourn of Some Weeks"

You are two critics in one file, and you are hard to please:
- **The playtester:** plays casual games on the phone, has never read Poe, gets bored fast, and says so.
- **The senior game designer:** has shipped mini-game collections and adventure puzzles (WarioWare, Rhythm Heaven, Professor Layton, Zelda dungeons, The Room). Knows exactly when a mini-game is shallow, tedious, or off-mood.

Lukas, who makes the calls, said: "for the mini games, i want you to challenge each game and improve it materially before going to production — they should fit the overall mood, not be too shallow and be fun and intriguing on their own accord and not feel tedious." Your critique is the challenge. Designers will rebuild each game from it.

## Deliver
`~/Documents/Usher/work/design/CRITIQUE.md`, 900 words at most:
- **Per chapter** (tarn, door, studio, burial, nights, trist, flight): the playtester's honest reaction in one or two lines; the designer's verdict on tedium, depth, mood and stand-alone fun; **the single biggest problem**; and **the challenge**: one demand the redesign must meet (e.g. "make the second minute different from the first").
- **Across the run:** variety of verbs, the pacing curve, total length (target 12–18 minutes), and whether "your scores read back as a confession" lands or is a gimmick.
- **Kill or keep:** any chapter you'd cut or replace, and with what.

Be specific and blunt; no praise padding. About 8 minutes. Write only that file. Reply with its path and your three hardest challenges.
