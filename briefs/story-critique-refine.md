# Story phase: critique and refinement (one round, sent to all four specialists)

This is time-boxed, so critique and revision happen in one pass. Work briskly.

**Context you didn't have before:** this game is a competition entry. The whole window is about 2.5 hours, most of it spent on the idea; the build will be a playable slice, possibly not every chapter. Lukas wants to blow the other entrants out of the water: the excellence lives in the idea and its translation into a game that is mysterious, exciting, fun, delightful, and wry and witty where it can be. Judge everything against that bar.

**What the coordinator sees across the four drafts:** all four dropped the reading-speed clock, and all four lean on the same claim: someone's silence, tact or care keeps Madeline buried. They're good, but side by side they read as four solemn games about complicity. The room needs one that is also *fun*: a pleasure to poke at, with a wit and a mystery that pull you forward. Push yours toward what only it can do, and toward delight.

## 2. Critique them

Write `~/Documents/Usher/work/story/critique-<your seat>.md` (narrator / madeline / house / reader), 300–500 words. For each of the other three: its **strongest idea**, its **weakest point** (as a game someone finishes and remembers, or as a reading of Poe), and any **misreading** of the tale, checked against `~/Documents/Usher/source/usher-1845.md` with [uNN] citations. Close with a two-sentence **Bet**: of all four, yours included, which you'd back to win the room, and why. Don't vote for yours by reflex.

## 3. Revise your own direction

Write `~/Documents/Usher/work/story/<NN>-<seat>-v2.md` (01-narrator / 02-madeline / 03-house / 04-reader). Leave your original unchanged.

- Fix what the others' work exposed in yours, and steal what makes yours better (credit it). **Stay distinct:** your direction at its strongest, not a blend of all four. If you're converging with another, push toward what only yours can do.
- Open with **What changed and why**, at most 120 words.
- Then the same ten sections as before (name and pitch, whose experience, the arc, the first minute, what the game can say that the tale can't, what only this tale could do, the seed, why it's small, the risk, a sample moment).
- Add an eleventh: **The slice.** If only 60–90 minutes of build exist, what one stretch of the game gets built so that it alone wins the room?
- 800–1,100 words in total. Shorter is better.

Touch no other file. When you're done, reply with both file paths, your Bet line, and a two-sentence summary of your direction as it now stands.
