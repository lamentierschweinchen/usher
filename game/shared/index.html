Every cut, on every chapter's light

Same cuts, same scale, eight lights. If a cut stops reading on one light, its profile is wrong, not the light.

Touchable

A lit edge where the lamp gets round the paper. Rest: nothing (phones: a faint rim). Hover or focus: a lamp rim and a 2-unit lift. Press: a hard white edge, pressed to the screen. Held (dragging): lifted off the screen, its shadow softening. The fifth is live: hover it, press it.

Lace density

Left: desktop, full lace. Right: setLace(.55), what autoLace() picks on phones and coarse pointers: fewer, larger curls, no hole under 3 units. Silhouettes keep their outlines; only the filigree thins.

Type on light

Black type on a lit card above water; paper type on a black ribbon over glass. Sound-words are cut from the same black paper, and turn red only in the tarn's truth.

The iris

A black stage with a lace-edged hole closes on the room (0.7 s), the intertitle holds, the hole opens (0.9 s). irisD(cx, cy, r) draws it; runIris(path, cx, cy, from, to, ms) runs it.
