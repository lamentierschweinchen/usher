Ink & Tarn

Ink line on bare paper, staged as a toy theatre. Below the waterline the house comes back as its own negative, and only the truth holds still.

Three lines

  1. The ideaInk on bare paper, staged as a toy theatre: flat black bands, blank-faced cut-outs, sound set as type. Under the waterline the house returns as its own negative, and only what really happened holds still, in red.
  2. Why it fits UsherThe tale turns on a man looking down at “the re-modelled and inverted images” [u02]. Beardsley’s Usher is already bare paper against flat black, and a silent film can print every sound instead of playing it.
  3. The riskRestraint can read as empty. Hairlines go grey at phone width and the house is small in the frame, so the look rests on drawing quality, and thin ink forgives nothing in an hour’s build.
