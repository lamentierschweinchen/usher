# Poe's voice

*Voice specialist, 31 October 2026. Marks: **V** verbatim, **R** recut (cut, re-addressed, or one word struck), **N** new in his manner. Ids from `source/usher-1845.md`.*

## 1. How the game speaks

**A. Recital.** Every line is Poe's own, recut, re-addressed or misread.
- *First minute:* "a letter from him — which, in its wildly importunate nature, had admitted of no other than a personal reply" [u03]. *A servant in waiting took my horse* [u07].
- *Only this tale:* the 1845 text runs under the game, line by line.
- *Risk:* Poe gave the servant, the physician, Madeline and the custom no words, so the narrator narrates everyone: a wall of text in instalments.

**B. Ventriloquy.** New lines in his diction and rhythm.
- *First minute:* "Come; I say come, for your society is the one remedy I have not tried."
- *Only this tale:* the lore can sound as though Poe wrote it.
- *Risk:* pastiche. There's nothing to recognise, every line must equal Poe's, and the flat line's twin is the purple one.

**C. Cut-outs.** Poe's sentences, cut at his own joints. New lines appear only where he is silent, made from his words.
- *First minute:* the letter: "— an earnest desire to see you, as my best, and indeed my only personal friend —" [u03]. Its P.S., mirror-written, can only be read in the water: *Look not down within the tarn.* Tap: *Such is the paradoxical law* [u05]. The banner: *Nevertheless, in this mansion of gloom I now proposed to myself a sojourn of some weeks* [u03].
- *Only this tale:* Poe builds his periods from dashes, so the dash becomes the tap. The cut is the trick: "the lady, at least while living, would be seen by me no more" [u15] reads one way above and another below. The voice is cut like the look: black paper.
- *Risk:* a collage only a Poe reader can parse. The rule and the test below guard against it.

**I'd pick C.** Poe speaks wherever he spoke, and there's a rule for where he didn't. Its wordplay is made of cuts and single struck words: the "simple deceptions" Lukas asked for, leaning on the author.

## 2. Voice sheet

**Rule.** Where Poe wrote it, cut it; don't reword it. Cut only at his joints (dash, semicolon, comma). You may change person or tense, or strike one word and write one. Where he's silent, use words from the tale and keep under twelve. *Test:* could it be a lost line of the 1845 text? One line on screen, two at a reveal.

- **The guest**, author of the Account: Poe's "I", long when wrong, short when right. Tells: *merely*, *surely*, *beyond doubt* [u36, u40]. Poe never says *naturally*.
- **Roderick:** Poe gives him two voices [u10]: the quaver ("I *must* perish") and the leaden concision (four words, full stop). When it hurts he calls himself "him" [u14]. He never says "she" until the door [u48].
- **The physician:** he gets only "obtrusive and eager inquiries" [u28], so he asks, or breaks off at a dash, and passes on [u07]. His only plain statements are a diagnosis and a count.
- **The valet:** silent [u07]. His copperplate tidies by deletion: harsh words out, an ellipsis in.
- **The servant and the peasant:** short, plain, right about time, in the tale's nouns (*some weeks*, *the family*).
- **Madeline** (revised): Poe gives her no words, so she has only the guest's, and only their ends. She cuts his line once and keeps the tail: the head is struck, the tail is hers. Back-slanted red, no dashes, no italics. Her name is her method: made line. The one exception is the lid.
- **The Account:** Poe's sentences verbatim, with your counts in the blanks. The labels flatter; the counts don't.
- **The interface:** the tale's verbs (*look down, rein in, harken, strike, let be*), numbers in words, Poe's nouns as labels. Never "tap", "score" or "retry".
- **Red:** *Say, rather:* followed by a Poe word or a count, never an explanation.

**Tics.** *Use:* the dash, as cut and pause; one italic per screen, on the true word (Poe italicises *must*, *heart*, *did*); the guest's "I say"; rhetorical questions, Roderick's alone. *Avoid* in the game's own voice: shudders and swoons (leave those to the player), *ghastly*, *hideous*, *horror*, and exclamation marks from anyone but Roderick. *Wry, not purple:* never add an adjective to Poe, only take them away. The joke is his own deadpan set after something enormous: "Such opinions need no comment, and I will make none" [u26].

## 3–4. Scene by scene: Poe's best, and every sample line rewritten

*Poe has* lists fragments that no rewrite uses. Rewrites run `before` → after.

**Letter and arrival.** *Poe has:* "acute bodily illness — a mental disorder which oppressed him" [u03], re-addressed into the letter; "no room for hesitation" [u03], taken literally: the button has none.
- `THE TARN would like a word.` / `Don't look in the tarn. Everyone does.` → the mirror-written P.S.: *Look not down within the tarn.* **R** [u05]. Reading it disobeys it. Tapped: *Such is the paradoxical law.* **R** [u05]
- `Turn back.` → *Rein in.* **R** [u02]
- `Nevertheless, … a sojourn of some weeks` → the whole sentence **V** [u03].
- `"Shall I stable him for some weeks, sir?"` → "For some weeks, sir?" **R** [u03]. "A night or two." stays flat, so the servant can be right.
- `"Twins, the family. Only ever one about."` → "Twins, the Ushers. One keeps the house. You'll see the other." **N**. *Keeps the house* reads first as housebound [u13], later as the keep [u29].

**I. Tarn.** *Poe has:* "It was possible, I reflected" [u02], as is; "the re-modelled and inverted images" [u02], as the caption.
- `"I'd have sworn."` → *What* must *have been a dream.* **R** [u06]
- `Not wrong. Early.` → *Not wrong. Not yet.* **N**. This plants *yet* for the lid.
- `Say, rather: 5.` → *Say, rather: five.* **R** [u48]

**First interlude.**
- `Not a difference. A trick of the water.` → *A fancy so ridiculous, indeed, that I but mention it.* **R** [u05]
- `a trick of the light, naturally` → *my excited fancy* **R** [u40]
- `"Odd. Not a curl on it."` → "A wild inconsistency. No web-work on it." **R** [u06]
- `"If the sister dies, send for me…"` → "Should the lady die, you will send for me? Before —" **N**. Then: *He accosted me with trepidation and passed on.* **V** [u07]
- Cards (the rest stand): `family custom` → *a harmless precaution* **R** [u28]; `coincidence` → *the coincidence alone* [u40], which wears down to *most extraordinary coincidence* [u45].
- `Unaccounted for` → *Unaccountable* **R** [u32]

**II. Door.** *Poe has:* "deficiency … of collateral issue" [u04], misread as "no side door".
- `"The family don't use the door, sir." "Then how do they get out?"` → "The family never venture forth, sir." **R** [u13] / "Then how do they go?" **N** (*go*: leave, or die).
- `P.S. in the hand of M.U. She wanted…` → the P.S. signs itself *M.U.*, and her echo strikes the Account (revised: see reveal 2). Cut the gloss.

**Gallery.** *Poe has:* "the stem of the Usher race … had put forth, at no period, any enduring branch" [u04].
- `___ went down` → *Descended: ___* **R** [u04]
- `"Twelve days. I counted."` → "Twelve days of my fortnight. I counted them." **N**. *My* is the clue.

**III. Studio.** *Poe has:* "the vaulted and fretted ceiling" [u08]; "without having noticed my presence, disappeared" [u14], as she crosses.
- `"You'd tell me if you heard anything?"` → "And you have not heard it? — you have not then heard it?" **R** [u34]. It foreshadows "Not hear it?" [u48].

**Collapse.** *Poe has:* "frequent although transient affections of a partially cataleptical character" [u15]; *affections*: illness, and love.
- `"Catalepsy. A trance that passes for death. It passes."` stays **N**.
- `"She is dead."` (twice) → "The lady Madeline is no more." … "No more." **R** [u28]

**IV. Burial.**
- `"You've got the heavy end?"` stays, under *We two alone bore it to its rest.* **V** [u29]
- `"It's in the book…"` / `"'Yet' means not yet."` → "Never screwed. It is written: *the yet unscrewed lid*." **R** [u30] / "'Yet' means not yet, surely." **N** / "Not in this house." stays.
- `"Before anyone does anything."` → "Before —" **N**, still unfinished.
- At the fourth screw: "Thus, thus, and not otherwise, shall I be lost." **V** [u12]
- `Well until the guest's arrival.` → *Steadily borne up. Succumbed the evening of the guest's arrival.* **R** [u15]
- The valet's `resting` stays: Poe's "rest" [u29].

**V. Nights.** *Poe has:* "Sleep came not near my couch — while the hours waned and waned away" [u32]; "certain low and indefinite sounds" [u32], as a slip's text.
- `"It was my turn. She went instead."` → "It was I who should have gone below. I dared not." **R** [u48]. Even in confession he leaves her out.

**Supper.** *Poe has:* "the most insipid food was alone endurable" [u11].
- `"Strike 'creaked'. Too loud."` → "Strike 'grating'. Too loud." **R** [u29]. At the door he says the word himself [u48].

**VI. Trist.** *Poe has:* captions: "uplifted his mace outright", "cracked, and ripped, and tore all asunder" [u39], "a shriek so horrid and harsh" [u43].
- `"Go on."` → "You will read, and I shall listen." **R** [u36]: the guest's own promise, mirrored.
- `"That's only the wind."` → *It was the work of the rushing gust.* **V** [u49]. True, and no use.
- `"the rushing gu—"` → *It was the work of the rushing gu—* **R** [u49], and nothing finishes it (revised). *Guest* is not Poe's word; leave it to the ear.

**Library.**
- `a fortnight, the lid unscrewed…` → *A fortnight, previously to final interment; the lid yet unscrewed; a friend to bear it, and to write.* **R** [u28–30]
- Below, every volume opens: *During the whole of a dull, dark, and soundless day in the autumn of the year* **V** [u02]. Poe never says which year.

**VII. Flight.** *Poe has:* "Oh whither shall I fly?" [u48]; "the vast house and its shadows were alone behind me" [u50], for looking back.
- `Say, rather: the tarn gave you back.` → *Say, rather: the tarn would not keep you.* **N**

**The Account.** *Poe has:* "lie within the compass of merely written words" [u17], as its motto.
- `taps hushed 41 · stones set 8 · Usher: 0` → *Some alleviation of his malady: forty-one* **R** [u03] · *A mere different arrangement: eight* **R** [u02] · *Ushers: none.* **N**

**The hinge.**
- `Cheerfulness of my society: considerable.` stays **R** [u03]. Her red cuts at *considerable* and keeps nothing, the only time she does (revised).
- `Nevertheless.` stays **V** [u03]. Poe gave it a comma; the full stop is ours. It is the guest's whole character in one word (dread, then in anyway), and on the turned side it becomes hers: she came up anyway. It's the one word neither hand corrects: never gloss it, never redden it.
- `The same evening, from below.` → *The evening of your arrival. Immediately beneath.* **R** [u15, u29]: beneath your bedroom.

**The act-two reveals** (revised to her echo). Each one strikes a line of the Account.
1. Tarn: *~~without~~ having noticed my presence.* **R** [u14]. She saw you first.
2. Door: *~~The writer spoke … of~~ an earnest desire to see me.* **R** [u03]. *See me*: visit me, or look at me. The P.S. was hers.
3. Burial: `His turn. I went.` → inside the lid, *You have put me living in his tomb.* **R** [u48]. Roderick's cry, turned round: his *we* was always him and you; *his tomb* says whose turn it was; *living* says catalepsy. The Account: ELEMENTS' *~~the yet un~~screwed lid.* **R** [u30] (revised). Your line is good Poe, but half of it is "So he said", and it explains where it should accuse. Keep its *Thus, thus* at the fourth screw, so the lid answers Roderick.
4. Library: *~~I hesitated not to acknowledge~~ how familiar was all this.* **R** [u07]

**The endings' last lines.**
1. *A Burial of Some Weeks*: `Guests: one. Say, rather…` → *I now proposed to myself a ~~sojourn~~ burial of some weeks.* **R** [u03]: the title's red word, in the title's hand, not hers (revised).
2. *The Swap* (no line yet) → *Such opinions need no comment, and I will make none.* **V** [u26]
3. *She Took the Horse*: `"It's been some weeks, ma'am."` → "Some weeks, ma'am." **R** [u03], then *~~A servant~~ in waiting took my horse.* **R** [u07] (revised)
4. *The Fall*: Poe's last sentence **V** [u50], the only ending without red.
5. *Considerable*: "Some weeks, then, sir." stays, then *Cheerfulness of my society: considerable.*, unstruck this time.
- Slot hints: `Someone heard her.` → *Said I not that my senses were acute?* **V** [u48].
- *say, rather: Reissiger* stays: Poe's "last waltz of Von Weber" [u17] is Reissiger's.
- `His heart is a lute…` → *His heart is a lute suspended; touch it, and it sounds.* The water's gloss says *Her*; both are correct, since French *son* is either. Replaces *Mon cœur*.
- `Five of five. The house thanks you.` → cut (revised): the tarn's reveal says it.

## 5. Raw material for wordplay

**Poe's equivocations.** "the quaint and equivocal appellation of the 'House of Usher' … both the family and the family mansion" [u04]; the tarn closes over the name, quotation marks and all [u50]. "ushered me into the presence of his master" [u07]: *usher* as a verb. "the entire family lay in the direct line of descent, and had always … so lain" [u04]. "she now stands without the door!" [u48]: outside it, or lacking it. "we will pass away this terrible night together" [u36]: spend it, or die. "collateral issue" [u04]: heirs, or a way out. *Son cœur … suspendu* [u01]: his heart or hers, hung up or suspended.

**Words with two senses, both in the tale.** *rest* (repose, a remainder, a silence in music [u29]); *lie* ("lies … beyond our depth" [u02]; Roderick "lying at full length" [u09]); *keep* (harmony [u04], prison tower [u29]); *vault* (ceiling [u08], tomb [u29]); *fretted* (carved, fitted with frets, worried [u08]); *mere* (merely, or a lake: "the mere house" [u02]); *bore* ("we two alone bore it to its rest" [u29], "bore him to the floor a corpse" [u49]); *grating* (a noise, or bars [u48]); *passages* (corridors [u07], text [u27]); *soundless* (silent, or too deep to sound [u02]); *still*, meaning calm or yet [u06, u26], which is how *yet* can mean "for ever"; *period* (an age, or a full stop [u12]); *fall* ("in the autumn of the year" [u02]); *Vigiliae* [u27], wakes; *Trist* [u37], tryst and *triste*; *catalepsy*, from Greek *kata*, "down". Not in the tale at all: *grave*, and *case* (except inside *casements* [u34]); *sojourn* appears once [u03], with no second sense.

**Person mirrors.** A glass swaps left and right; this game's mirror swaps *I* and *you*, *his* and *her*: "I will read, and you shall listen" [u36]; "*We have put her living in the tomb!*" [u48]; "would be seen by me no more" [u15].

**Letter mirrors.** The text has only two palindromes: *eye* ("the vacant and eye-like windows" [u02]) and *did*, italicised at her entrance ("there *did* stand" [u49]). Reversals: *evil/live* [u24], *keep/peek* [u29], *pool/loop* [u05], *reined/denier* [u02], *door/rood*, *room/moor*, *stars/rats* [u35], *was/saw* [u02]. Hidden words: *Usher* holds *she* and *her*; *hermit* [u39] holds *her*, and Roderick re-reads the hermit's door as "her coffin" [u48]; *Madeline* is made line. One letter apart: *gust/guest* [u49], *tomb/tome*, *tarn/turn*.

## After reading the others

**Adopt.** From ELEMENTS, *Echo*: she can only give back the end of the guest's line. It's Poe's own move ("Not hear it? — yes, I hear it" [u48]), gives her a verb, and keeps us honest: the tail must already be his. From SHADOWS, *A Mere Different Arrangement*, which is the narrator's own theory [u02]. Its prompt can be his sentence, and the Account names each shadow in his words (*the draperies* [u32], not "a coat").

**Kill.** In ELEMENTS, the echoes that only strip *un-* ("mentioned.", "accounted for."): a first-timer won't feel a lost prefix. Keep the tails that turn a whole clause. In SHADOWS, *Held to the Lamp*: a wall of text, read backwards through a page. The library already has Poe's shelf of titles [u27], read at a glance.

**Madeline: one rule.** *She has no words but the guest's, and only their ends: she cuts his line once and keeps the tail.* The head is struck; the tail is hers. My one-word swaps go; the lid stays her only sentence. It costs me "~~only~~ latest"; the echo is truer. (Revised in the body.)
1. At the hinge, after **Nevertheless.**: *~~I know not~~ how it was.* [u02]. His first evasion, answered.
2. Tarn: *~~without~~ having noticed my presence.* [u14]. She saw you first.
3. Library: *~~I hesitated not to acknowledge~~ how familiar was all this.* [u07]. Every generation had a guest.

**Red.** If red is hers alone (ELEMENTS B), the water's *Say, rather* lines become mirror-writing and Roderick's supper strike is in ink. The words stand.
