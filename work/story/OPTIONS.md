# Story: options for Lukas (2026-10-26)

Distilled by the coordinator from the four v2 directions and the four critiques in this folder.

## 1. Perfect Keeping (house, 03-house-v2.md)
**Pitch:** You are the House of Usher, a vain aesthete in stone, catching the sounds made inside you and playing them back to score your guest's readings. Lately the best material comes from the vault.
**First minute:** A cross-section of the house at dusk above its upside-down twin in the tarn. Poke a tapestry and it answers in Poe's word, *rustled*; the word drifts up, and you catch it into the crack, labelled like a museum card. The valet *ushers* a rider in. The house is delighted.
**Only Usher:** The climax is already sound design: three sentences read aloud, three noises answering [u39–u47]. Here you make those "coincidences", and on the third, a word you never caught rises from the vault: *someone else is playing you*.
**Risk:** The toy swallows the turn: her *stirs* becomes just another good sample. It's also the most to build in 75 minutes (drag, catch, timing).

## 2. Nevertheless (reader, 04-reader-v2.md)
**Pitch:** Usher as a cheap 1840s gamebook that turns down every choice in Poe's own words, until the stormy night you read aloud the silliest book in the house, whose choices work, on a door under the floor.
**First minute:** "To turn thy horse for home, turn to 2." Everyone does. §2, entire: "Nevertheless, in this mansion of gloom I now proposed to myself a sojourn of some weeks." The laugh lands in 20 seconds; the crack starts as your path through the numbers, zigzagging down the page.
**Only Usher:** No other gothic tale contains its own bad game (door, dragon, loot, a rule in rhyme on a shield [u42]), read over a real escape and then glossed, triumph by triumph, as her coffin breaking [u48].
**Risk:** It looks like "just a Twine", and the refusal joke can run dry before the dread arrives.

## 3. The Coincidence Alone (Madeline, 02-madeline-v2.md)
**Pitch:** You are Madeline, buried alive under a man who can explain anything; you have one stormy night to make a noise he can't.
**First minute:** Torchlight, two faces above your coffin, one of them yours. You strain to show you're alive; the text answers: "the mockery of a faint blush… so terrible in death", and files its first excuse in the margin. The screws turn, one for each word.
**Only Usher:** His calming book is your weapon: land a sound on the Mad Trist's own words, and his excuses wear down from "the coincidence alone" to nothing, until Poe's italic *did* at the door [u49].
**Risk:** If the trick (sync to his reading) doesn't click, it plays as a quick-time event.

*Cut:* the narrator's "Merely Electrical Phenomena" (01-narrator-v2.md), a reverse detective game of explaining things away. It's the same contest as option 3, seen from the explainer's side, and it has the weaker aha.

**All three slices are the same night:** the Mad Trist, through to the door, about 5–8 minutes.

**The seed:** all four specialists dropped "read too fast" on their own. It punishes the attentive reading your edition protects, and in Poe the fall *is* the finish. The crack survives in every option.

**Coordinator's pick:** Perfect Keeping. It's the only one where the fun and the horror are one act, the most original game on the table, and two of the four specialists backed it, including one rival.
