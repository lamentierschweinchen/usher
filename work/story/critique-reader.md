# Critique · reader seat (04)

## 01 · The Cheerfulness of My Society (narrator)

**Strongest idea.** Poe's own explaining sentence is the lit, one-click default. "It was, beyond doubt, the coincidence alone —" setting itself under your finger is the best trap in the room.

**Weakest point.** A dozen identical forks between explaining and attending change nothing but a grade in the margin. The draft names its own risk ("morality quiz") and doesn't escape it. Nobody tells a friend about a game that graded their tact.

**Misreadings.** In [u32] the draperies take the blame for his *nervousness*, not for her sounds, which go unexplained and overpower him ("unaccountable yet unendurable"). The temptation also depends on explanations that "genuinely help". Poe says the opposite: "the futility of all attempt at cheering" [u16]. The design needs cheer to work, and the tale says it never did.

## 02 · Without the Door (Madeline)

**Strongest idea.** Input comes before text: you act, then a sentence arrives to explain you away. That reverses the narrator's order, in which the book is the original and her coffin "the echo" [u40]. The screws giving way one word at a time is the best sample of the four, and the change from *Mon* to *Son cœur* is a real find.

**Weakest point.** It spends the tale's only mystery in the first minute. You *are* the living woman, so "is she alive?" never gets asked. After that, two-thirds of the game is holding still in the dark, as the draft concedes.

**Misreading.** Nothing serious. "The only one who acts" stretches a week that Roderick hears as "first feeble movements" [u48], but that is an interpretation, not an error.

## 03 · Perfect Keeping (house)

**Strongest idea.** Sounds become written words rising through the drawing, and you can hold each one in the masonry or let it reach Roderick's ear. It is the only verb of the four a stranger would enjoy before understanding it. The title's double "keeping" [u04, u29] is the best name in the room.

**Weakest point.** Two gauges and a stream of tokens invite optimizing. The early-fall ending also brings back, as a punishment, the fail state the room has just argued away.

**Misreading.** The verb fights the tale's central fact, which is that Roderick heard her: "I heard them — many, many days ago" [u48]. A house that can keep her sounds from him makes his confession false. An early fall "still holding her" deletes [u49], the one scene the whole tale is built to deliver. A minor slip: "even the most trivial, incident" [u12] concerns events working on his soul, not his senses [u11].

## Bet

I'd back 03 once its gauges are cut: a word you can hold or let go is the most playable thing in the room, and it builds in an hour. Its solemn thesis is the cost, and the room still needs a joke, which is what my revision tries to supply.
