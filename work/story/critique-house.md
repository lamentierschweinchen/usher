# Critique from the house seat (03, systems designer)

## 01 · The Cheerfulness of My Society (narrator)

**Strongest idea:** Poe's own explaining sentence sits lit, one click away, so composure is the easy move. Pairing tact [u45] with fear [u48] is the sharpest reading of the narrator any of us made.

**Weakest point:** the verb is *don't click the obvious button*. Everyone reaches the same end, the only record of play is the colour of the margin, and nothing in it is a pleasure to poke. It also casts the player as the least interesting person in the house.

**Misreading:** he doesn't "blame her sounds on the draperies" [u32]. The draperies explain his *nervousness* before any sound arrives. When the "low and indefinite sounds" come, he explains nothing: "Overpowered by an intense sentiment of horror", he gets up and paces. The explaining comes at [u36] and [u40], so beat 5's core move isn't Poe's. Also, the "childish experiment" is looking into the tarn [u05]; rearranging the scene is only a hypothesis [u02]. 04 makes the same slip.

## 02 · Without the Door (Madeline)

**Strongest idea:** *input before text*: you act, and a sentence arrives to explain you away. Reversing [u40] so the book becomes her echo is the best single translation of Poe into play among the four. The twin face looking down [u30] is exact.

**Weakest point:** stillness, as the direction admits. For two-thirds of the game the player lies in a box with one input. The climb thrills, but late.

**Misreading:** nothing material. "The only person in Usher who acts" overstates it. Roderick resolves on the entombment [u28] and both men carry her down [u29], and the overstatement lets the men off as bystanders.

## 04 · I Continued the Story (reader)

**Strongest idea:** the page *is* the house. A justified paragraph makes the wall and its inverted twin the tarn. The fissure is a river of white through the type, and the footnote asterisk is the only star in a starless night [u35]. The wittiest images in the room, with the Trist as a decoy quest [u42].

**Weakest point:** the only power is to go on, and the moral act is to stop. A game whose best move is the bookmark leaves no one a memory of doing anything.

**Misreading:** "as if … there had been found the potency of a spell" [u49] is said of Roderick's *utterance*, his "Madman!", not of the narrator's reading. The tale's last spell is spoken, not read, which weakens "your reading is the fall."

## Bet

I'd back the house as revised in v2, because it is the only direction where the player's fun (scoring a bad romance with the house's own sounds) and the tale's horror are the same act. Its slice, the Mad Trist performed until a sound comes that you didn't play, is a complete set piece; if the room rewards beauty over play, back 04, which has the best first screen and no verb.
