# A Sojourn of Some Weeks: spine and chapters (coordinator draft, 2026-10-26)

Lukas's call: a Zelda-style adventure, with a main plot and a different mini-game at each turn of the story. You enter the House of Usher, solve something to open the door, then play through the tale (loosely, with licence), meeting people and challenges on the way. This draft assembles the spine from the concept sprint's ideas.

## Working title
**A Sojourn of Some Weeks** (the narrator's own understatement [u03]).

## You
The guest. An old schoolfriend, summoned by a frantic letter from Roderick Usher to cheer him up. You're kind, sensible, and very good at explaining things away.

## The spine
One beautiful screen: the house in cutaway, ink on paper, standing over its upside-down twin in the black tarn. You walk room to room, and people talk in short exchanges: the valet "of stealthy step", the physician with his "low cunning", Roderick, and Madeline passing silently. Each turn of the plot is a door into a different little game. Between chapters, glance down: **the reflection runs one step ahead and shows what's really happening** (from *Keeping House*).

## Chapters (each its own genre)
0. **The tarn.** Arrival at dusk. One easy *spot-the-difference* between the house and its reflection: the fissure is only in the water. (Tutorial: look down.)
1. **The door.** A *sliding-stone puzzle* of the façade. In the reflection, the gap you keep pushing around has a woman in it (from *The Arrangement*). Solve it and the door opens.
2. **The studio.** *Whack-a-mole* for the most sensitive man alive: hush sunbeams, roses and brass bands before they reach Roderick. Amid the rush, a small *tap.* from below (from *Hush*). Madeline crosses the room; nobody mentions her.
3. **The burial.** *Tidying game*: screw down the lid. It won't stay shut (from *Keeping House*). *(Sealed in the slice.)*
4. **The nights.** *Explain-it-away*: stamp each noise under your bed EXPLAINED ("the draperies", "the storm") until your stamps run out (from *Merely Electrical Phenomena*). *(Sealed in the slice.)*
5. **The Mad Trist.** You read Roderick a romance, and the game becomes a cheerful *8-bit quest*: Ethelred smashes the hermit's door, slays the dragon, wins the shield. In the tarn each blow is her breaking out, and the dragon is her brother.
6. **The flight.** The doors open on her; you run across the causeway as the house splits along the fissure and sinks.

## The trick
Every game you win, you win for the house. At the end, your scores are read back as a confession: *taps hushed 41 · stones set 8 · coincidences 2 · Usher: 0.*

## For everyone / for connoisseurs
Six quick, familiar games and one ghost story that's quietly keeping score. Every challenge, line and twist comes out of the tale, bent; knowing Poe makes each one land twice, and the know-it-all gets caught filling the last blank too fast.

## The slice (about 60 minutes, four builders in parallel)
The spine and chapters 0, 1, 2, 5 and 6. Chapters 3 and 4 appear as sealed doors on the map: "chapters to come."
