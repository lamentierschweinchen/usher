# 05 · Hush (a game for Roderick Usher)

**Pitch:** Whack-a-mole for the most sensitive man alive: silence every sunbeam, rose and creak before it reaches Roderick Usher, and score best for hushing the tapping in the vault.

**Genre:** Gothic whack-a-mole.

**The screen:** A dollhouse cutaway: Roderick's studio at the top, the narrator's bedroom at the bottom, above the vault [u29]. Every sensation is visible: light a red slant, scent a curl, sound a typeset word ("creak", "TAN-TARA") crawling up the stair toward his ear. Click to hush. The fissure is your life bar [u06].

**The first minute:** His torments arrive [u11]: a sunbeam (a drape drops), a rose (it wilts), a peppered dish (gruel), a brass band (shrinks to a lute's "plink"). Miss one: his gossamer hair rises, the crack inks lower. Amid the rush, a small "tap." in the vault. Click: "Hushed. Roderick thanks you." The taps never stop, each needs one more click, and each hush makes Roderick hide his face.

**The twist:** You are why she's still down there. Roderick heard her for days and "dared not speak" [u48]; the game makes you his silence, and pays you for it. Then the narrator reads the Mad Trist [u39–u47], and Poe's order inverts: each noise arrives *before* its line, and the romance rewrites itself to fit her. The last word is "step", on the stair. Clicking does nothing. The doors open [u49], the crack reaches the water, the tarn closes [u50]. Ledger: sunbeams 9, roses 4, trumpets 3, *her* 41. High score.

**For everyone:** A fast, silly tap game that quietly makes you an accomplice.

**For connoisseurs:** Every pest is on Roderick's list, the taps start long before anyone reads aloud, and Poe's coincidence runs backwards.

**The 60-minute slice:** One SVG cutaway; eight pest types as clickable tokens; hush counts; fissure health; a scripted three-minute run (pests → taps → Trist → "step" → collapse → ledger). Optional: a code-made guitar pluck per hush, the one sound he can bear.

**The risk:** If act one isn't genuinely frantic and funny, the turn has nothing to betray.

## Runners-up

- **Ethelred × retro dungeon crawler:** A cheerful 8-bit quest (smash the hermit's door, slay the dragon, win the shield) whose reflection in the tarn shows what each blow really is: Madeline breaking out of her tomb, and the dragon is her brother.
- **The valet "of stealthy step" × stealth game:** You play the servant who literally ushers [u07], sneaking gruel and guests past Roderick's hearing cones, until a second set of footsteps starts following your route exactly.
- **Madeline × precision platformer:** Celeste's heroine is also a Madeline with a dark mirror-self; this one wall-jumps from the vault to the room above, and every move lands upstairs as a noise in the romance being read aloud.
