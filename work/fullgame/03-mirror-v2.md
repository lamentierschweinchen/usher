# Blueprint 03 v2: the mirror

## What changed and why

The others exposed my thin interludes and my liars without tells. Taken, with credit: from the house (01), the missing-lace tell and the guest who fills a blank if you dither; from the account (02), the house keeping your excuse overnight. All three of us converged on red as contested, a flattering tarn and the P.S. as hers, and 02 also replays your recording. So I pushed where only the mirror goes: **it runs both ways.** What she does below rewrites act one. New: alternating portraits, and a sharper milestone.

## 1. Name and pitch

**A Sojourn of Some Weeks**, reflected as **A Burial of Some Weeks**. Explain away the House of Usher, then turn it over and play what you explained, against yourself, until it comes out differently.

## 2. The shape: 84 minutes

- **Act one, *Sojourn*, the guest above (51):** letter 2, tarn 5, door 6, studio 6, burial 7, nights 7, trist 5, flight 4, house 6, Account 2, hinge 1.
- **Act two, *Burial*, Madeline below (33):** tarn 3, door 4, studio 4, burial 5, nights 5, trist 4, flight and ending 5, house 3.

Each chapter learns and complicates above and turns below: 8–12 minutes. Act one ends whole; then **Nevertheless.** turns the stage over.

## 3. Between chapters

**Above: look, explain, sleep on it.** Hold to tip the view into the tarn, where two or three things differ (a portrait, a door, a lid). Pin one, then explain it with an excuse card ("the draperies") or leave it be. Dither ten seconds and the guest explains it, sensibly and wrongly (01). Overnight the house keeps your excuse (02): the drape really hangs there, and Roderick's guitar steadies. Leave it be, and Roderick tells you one true thing.

**Below: knock, and be explained.** Each excuse is a thing in Madeline's way (a nailed drape, a screw); each thing you left be is an opening. She knocks; in the water above, your recorded self answers with your old excuse. Find a gap in your alibis and the recording breaks: that act-one line changes.

## 4. Truth and deceit

One lie and one tell each:
- **Your Account and the scores** count truly and name falsely. Tell: *merely*, *naturally* (01).
- **The house** edits what you aren't watching and keeps your excuses. Tell: its repairs have no lace, and nothing old here is that clean [u06] (01).
- **The tarn** is true about everyone but the looker, whom it flatters. It inverts; look twice and it ripples, swapping two details. Tell: if you're in the picture, that part is vanity.
- **Roderick** leaves things out. **The physician** is honest, with a face lit for lying. **The valet's** candle marks where something happened.

The trap: house and tarn are one witness ("reduplication" [u26]). In act two she's the looker, so her account of herself is the one thing you can't check.

**Red means "not so":** a contradiction, never who's right; the water's hand above, hers below. Her edits have a gauze rim, so replaying act one shows which cheats were hers.

## 5. The mystery

From minute one, the P.S. is in another hand and flips the right way round as you read it.

Act one assembles the custom: one twin per generation is laid in the vault for a fortnight. In each portrait pair one face is painted out, on alternating sides by generation, and in the water it's the other face that's missing: each house shows the twin who lives in it. The vault's old lids have screw holes and no screws.

In act two, the fortnight is a shift: the buried twin keeps the house in the water, then climbs up as the other goes down. The lid is never screwed ("the yet unscrewed lid" [u30]). You screwed it, so act one's best burial score is the crime. The P.S. was hers: Roderick sent for you because you never hear anything, Madeline because you always look.

Left open: did Roderick fear her, or going down? Did she come up to swap, or to stay?

## 6. Characters

- **The guest:** his last excuse, the gust [u49], is true and useless. Below, he's your recording until she breaks it.
- **Roderick:** his guitar is the relationship. Excuses tune it (comfort), which eases act one and hardens act two; unexplained things make him talk (candour).
- **Madeline:** silent above; below, she writes in mirror-hand on the glass.
- **The physician** looks the villain and is the custom's honest witness; **the valet** serves whoever is upstairs.

## 7. The seven chapters, grown up

Idle, and the guest or the house decides. The stake above is act two: every hush, stamp and screw becomes her wall.
- **I Tarn.** Five differences, the cheat, then your flattering reflection, to claim or not. *Turn:* she redraws the house to make you look twice. The cheat was hers.
- **II Door.** Still until you move; three façades; the last forces a choice: whole house, or her route. *Turn:* she's the gap, and your old self chases her with stones.
- **III Studio.** Taps fall on the guitar's third beat; then, "You'd tell me if you heard anything?" *Turn:* under the floor, against your old hushes.
- **IV Burial.** Let the physician look or not; turn four screws, or leave by the door. *Turn:* carried, she bears her own weight or kicks for him, then backs out each screw.
- **V Nights.** Kept whole. *Turn:* the smuggler's side: she writes slips your cards can't stamp.
- **VI Trist.** Draw the blows; the echoes come first; you may close the book. *Turn:* now the page turns: her climb, your strokes on her lid, no mercy rule.
- **VII Flight.** Three stretches; sinking restarts one. *Turn:* her causeway, the house falling upward.

## 8. The mirror act

Each game gets a `mirror` mode on its existing reflected art, reading `api.ghost`; your recording plays in the water above her. Wherever she gets past him, that act-one result is rewritten, and the final Account is co-written: your numbers, her red. It reveals the swap, the screws, the P.S. and who carried the coffin.

## 9. Endings

Act two can rescue what act one spent.
- **The Fall** (Poe's): she reaches her brother's door. Default.
- **The Horse:** leave three differences open above, or break through three below, and she takes your horse.
- **The Swap:** her knock is heard (through your candour above, or a broken hush below), and Roderick goes down himself.
- **A Burial of Some Weeks:** you looked down every interlude and left a screw out. Your recorded glance meets her eye, and the guest goes down: *Guests: one. Say, rather: some weeks.*

## 10. Music and sound

Von Weber's "last waltz" (really Reissiger's) plays above; below, inverted and backwards. "The Haunted Palace" gains a stanza per chapter. The nights stay silent.

## 12. The risk

Act two feels like a rerun after an ending. Guard: every turn opens on a reveal, with your recording on screen to beat. Test: does Lukas say "that's me" and want to break it?

## 13. A sample moment

After the burial, in the water, three carry the coffin: Roderick at the head, Madeline at the foot carrying herself, and you, tall and gallant, in the middle. *Bearers: two,* says the Account. You're in the picture, so that part is vanity. "You've got the heavy end?" "I thought you had."

In act two you lie in the coffin. Roderick takes the head; at the foot, bare feet, yours now. Ahead, the guest carries a torch, narrating. The line rewrites itself in her hand: *Bearers: two. Say, rather: we two alone. He held the lamp.*
