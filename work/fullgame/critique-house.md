# Critique from the house: 02 and 03

## 02 · The Account
**Strongest idea.** The house keeps what you write: overnight it edits itself to match your page, so by morning a lie is "technically true". The sample's empty third frame is the best deceit in any of the three blueprints. Close behind: Roderick reading your page at supper.

**Weakest point.** As an evening it's a diary: the same gather–write–supper–night loop, seven days running. That's the critic's "same rhythm seven times", now with homework added. The mystery is delivered on a schedule (Day 1 to Day 6), and the secret is Poe's own, a twin buried alive. All three of us found its extra twist, the earlier guests.

**Deceit.** It's delightful when the house rewrites itself, and fair, because each edit stays "lamp-rimmed for a day". But *sworn* (a fact two differently-lying witnesses agree on is true) works as an oracle. By Day 2, truth is a lookup, which is exactly what Lukas ruled out.

**Build risk.** High. The overnight art has to answer every combination of blanks, Cheer and Candour have to be tuned against each other, and Act II needs your whole run recorded. The milestone is honest, but it proves the loop rather than the delight.

## 03 · The Mirror
**Strongest idea.** Act II is played against your recorded Act I (`api.ghost`). Every hush, stamp and screw becomes her wall, and "the cheat you caught was hers" changes what Act I meant. It also has the best textual find of the round: "the yet unscrewed lid" [u30], which turns your best burial score into the crime.

**Weakest point.** Act I is barely longer than today's game: chapters of four to seven minutes, with six minutes of house in fifty-one. The first hour is still seven toys on a map, now as setup for a rerun. The pull is "what will this become?", not a question you're asking.

**Deceit.** The most delightful of the three, because it works in hindsight: Act II rewrites Act I. That's only fair if her edits are visibly seeded in Act I. "Look twice and two details swap" is arbitrary.

**Build risk.** The highest: seven mirror modes driven by recorded logs, and an idle Act I makes a feeble ghost. The studio milestone, though, is exactly the right one-day proof.

## Common ground
All three of us redefined red as disagreement, put earlier guests in the portraits, and caught that the waltz is Reissiger's. Those now belong to everyone and are no one's edge.

## Bet
I'd build 03, with the house's register filling its interludes. Playing against your own recorded evening is the one idea here that nothing else can supply, and its studio milestone proves it in a day; a searchable house can be grafted onto it, but the ghost can't be grafted onto mine without turning mine into 03.
