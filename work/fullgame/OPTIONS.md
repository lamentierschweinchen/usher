# The full game: options for Lukas (2026-10-27)

Distilled from three blueprints (house, account, mirror), their cross-critiques and their v2s. All three designers independently converged on: red as *contested* (it marks a disagreement, never who's right); a tarn that leans true but inverts, ripples and flatters whoever is looking; the P.S. in Madeline's hand; and the family secret of one twin per generation laid in the vault. **All three bet on the mirror**, including both rivals.

## 1. The Mirror (03-mirror-v2.md)
**Pitch:** Spend an evening explaining away the House of Usher; then turn it over and play what you explained, as Madeline, against your own recorded self, until it comes out differently.
**First minute:** the letter, whose P.S. is in another hand and flips the right way round as you read it. Then the tarn: five differences, a cheat, and your own flattering reflection to claim or not.
**Only Usher:** the house and its "reduplication" in the tarn [u26] are the two acts. Every hush, excuse and screw you chose above becomes a wall in her way below, and wherever she gets past your recording, act one's Account is rewritten in her red. "The yet unscrewed lid" [u30]: act one's best burial score is the crime.
**Risk:** act two feels like a rerun after an ending, and seven mirror modes is the biggest build.

## 2. An Account of My Sojourn (02-account-v2.md)
**Pitch:** Whatever you write down, the house keeps, while the guest in you fills any blank you hesitate over, sensibly and wrongly.
**First minute:** the arrival, then the first evening page: "His sister is ___", with the letter's three crossed-out words (dying, quiet, resting) as the choices. The guest fills it for you if you dither. Roderick reads it at supper; by morning she crosses the studio exactly as you wrote her.
**Only Usher:** Madeline isn't dying; she's being written. The tale is the guest's account, and here the account is the weapon.
**Risk:** a writing desk seven times over, close to the "blocks of text and decisions" you rejected.

## 3. The Direct Line (01-house-v2.md)
**Pitch:** Search the cutaway house and its reflection, matching silhouette profiles to rebuild the family register; learn why the Ushers are always twins and only one stays up.
**First minute:** the letter in three hands; then the gallery: a portrait pair, one face scrubbed, and in the water the scrubbed face is a squat, hook-nosed profile you'll meet on the stair.
**Only Usher:** the "direct line" [u04] is bookkeeping, and the physician "of the family" [u07] is family: he went down, and came up.
**Risk:** homework: dates and registers, with the mirror as a short coda.

**Coordinator's pick:** The Mirror, with the house's profile mystery (the physician is family) folded into its interludes.
