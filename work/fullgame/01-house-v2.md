# The Direct Line, v2

**What changed and why.** 02 and 03 exposed v1's reskinned mirror act, its oracle guitar, and its single rhythm of search. Now:
- The house **keeps your wrong entries** overnight (from 02).
- The guitar **flatters on Roderick's own page**.
- Three long searches, not seven equal ones.
- Act II searches the register's other half against your **recorded Act I** (03's ghost).
- 03's "yet unscrewed lid" [u30] gives Roderick a motive.

To stay distinct, the deduction turns on identity by profile, the art's own rule.

## 1. Name and pitch
**The Direct Line.** Match profiles across the house and its reflection to fill the Usher register: why twins, and why does only one stay up? Then search from below, as the one who went under.

## 3. Between chapters: look down, write it down
- **What changed.** Three differences; one is the house tidying.
- **Long searches.** Tap a lit room for a close-up (portraits, the *Vigiliae* [u27], paintings [u18], the vault wall); drag the waterline down for its reflection. Collect words, dates and **profiles**. One exchange: *cheer* or *press*.
- **The register.** A row per generation: who stayed up, who went down, and the day they came up. Match profiles across house, water and living cast. Leave a blank ten seconds and the guest fills it. Three right and Roderick's guitar resolves. Overnight the house keeps what's written (02): leave a wrong entry and its evidence is tidied away.

## 4. Truth and deceit
**Red means contested:** two witnesses disagree, and it never says which is right.
- **The guest** explains. *Tell:* speed, and *merely*, *naturally*, *no doubt*.
- **The house** tidies while you look down, and nightly to match your page. *Tell:* a repair has no lace (too clean for this house [u06]), plus wax, whose we never say.
- **The tarn** leans true but *inverts* (sides, text, roles), *ripples* (in wind it shows another moment: spreading rings, to come; closing, past) and *flatters* (carrying her down looks like rescue). The valet is never in it.
- **Roderick** speaks false and sings true.
- **The guitar** judges honestly, except on his own page, 1809, where it resolves for the kindest answer. *Tell:* it resolves too soon.
- **The physician:** true facts, unstated motive.
- **The scores:** true counts under flattering labels.

Each rule is taught in a set piece first.

## 5. The mystery
The house stands on one twin per generation lying beneath it. At "the fortnight" [u28] one is laid alive in the vault, lid unscrewed by custom [u30]; whoever climbs out is painted out. A guest carries and explains.
- **I–II.** Paired portraits, one face scrubbed; one birth registered per generation.
- **III–IV.** Vault tallies run to fourteen; one set stops at twelve. In the water, 1775's scrubbed face is the physician's: "the physician of the family" [u07] is family.
- **V–VI.** Roderick's lit tunnel [u18] is this vault from inside. In 1809 *his* face is scrubbed: he climbed out at twelve ("I doubted to whom I spoke" [u09]). He screws her lid down lest the house want him back. The letter's three hands: Roderick's *dying*, Madeline's *quiet*, copperplate's *resting*.
- **Act II.** Every generation had a guest.

**Reveal:** in the nights, if he trusts you: "I know the sound. I made it." Otherwise at Poe's door [u48].

## 6. Characters
- **The guest:** his auto-fill is what you learn to overrule; he's a role, not a man.
- **Roderick:** cheer and complicity raise his trust; pressing and the physician lower it. It shows in pose, playing, and his visit in V. Friendship rewards complicity; only his songs show what to break.
- **Madeline:** cargo, author, player.
- **The physician:** everyone's suspect, and family.
- **The valet:** the house's hand; no reflection.

## 7. The seven chapters, grown up
Idling only lets the house tidy, at your cost. No mercy rules. Each chapter ends on a visible fork.
- **Tarn.** The house undoes one of five differences; only missing lace betrays it. Count the lit window: the gallery opens.
- **Door.** Two cracks, and the water swaps sides, so the true branch looks wrong. The shortcut leaves a stone askew and the library shut.
- **Studio.** Roderick talks while you hush, and sings only in silence. KNOCK: "Spare me everything?" Hush it, or let it through for one true sentence.
- **Burial.** Keep the lure and the feet. Let the physician look: a casebook page. Roderick insists on four screws.
- **Nights.** Keep "Quite so." Persuaded slips become entries the house defends. Then Roderick's lamp: trusting you, he confesses, and you go down.
- **Trist.** Keep the page turn. Missed beats become scratches, the only words she'll have. At the threshold, mace raised, gust leaning: strike, or let the wind.
- **Flight.** The storm search, then three runs: what you hushed, your false entries, her.

## 8. The mirror act: the register's other half
Tap the red *Burial*: you're Madeline in `C.returned`, set upright. Above, your recorded Act I self (03's `api.ghost`) reads your Account aloud. Each wrong entry the house kept is a wall; each right one, a door.
- **Drowned house (6).** The scrubbed twins testify ("It's very restful"; their tallies disagree). Behind every portrait, a top-hatted guest. Match the profiles: all yours.
- **Nights (4).** Forge a noise none of your ghost's stamped excuses explains.
- **Burial (3).** Hold each screw as your ghost turns it.
- **Flight (3).** Move only while he looks back.

It fills the guest's row and finds the custom's last clause: the water needs something under it, not someone.

## 9. Endings
1. **Perfect Keeping.** Keep the whole custom: the house stands, Poe's tale never happens, you sign the register. "Some weeks, then, sir."
2. **The Fall.** Poe's, the default.
3. **She Took the Horse.** Spare her once and get 1809 right before the trist: Roderick goes down in her place.
4. **The Fragments of the "House of Usher".** Act II only, every row right, yours included. Drag the title's words into the tarn; it closes over the name, quotation marks and all [u50]. Both titles read *Sojourn*. No red.

## 10. Music and sound
The guitar (Karplus-Strong) judges, and flatters on its own page. "The Haunted Palace" adds a verse per chapter when he trusts you. Reissiger's "last waltz" detunes chapter by chapter and runs backwards in Act II. Silence before every tap. Every cue is also visible.

## 12. The risk
Homework. And with red, the guests and Reissiger now common to all three, the delight must come from the deduction. Test: "What lied to you, and how did you know?"

## 13. A sample moment
You drag the waterline over the fifth portrait pair. Above: a gentleman, 1775, beside a frame scrubbed black. In the water his frame is empty; the scrubbed one holds a squat, hook-nosed profile. You look up: the scrubbed frame holds a pleasant landscape, and your pen has written *Merely a landscape.* But its trees have no lace, and there's wax on the frame. You strike your own sentence. On the stair, the physician pretends to look for his hat. You drag his profile into the last blank: *___ went down.* Two rooms away, Roderick's guitar, hung on one note all evening, resolves. The physician, without turning: "Twelve days. I counted."
