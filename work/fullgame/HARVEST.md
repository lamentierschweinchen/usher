## 1. Include, improved

### Prologue and letter
- **The letter:** "My sister is ~~dying~~ ~~quiet~~ resting"; P.S. "Don't look in the tarn. Everyone does." (ST); three hands (H).
  → One hand per word: Roderick, Madeline, the valet's copperplate. The P.S. is mirror-written, legible only in the tarn, so you look down first.
- **Turn back:** "To turn thy horse for home…", refused with "Nevertheless … a sojourn of some weeks" [u03] (s4, P).
  → One refused choice at the causeway. Never try, and the game "had a page ready for that. It will keep it." (P): Madeline finds it below.
- **"Shall I stable him for some weeks, sir?" "A night or two."** (ST).
  → *Some weeks* is the refrain the endings resolve.
- **noscript:** "This book requires its reader to have scripts enabled. Nevertheless." (P). → Ship it.

- **Pin and explain** (M). → Too thin. Open each interlude on H's *what changed overnight*: three differences, one the house tidying; chapter I's verb, so no teaching.

- **The guest fills a blank if you dither** (H, A). → After ten seconds, *merely*: sensible, wrong, overrulable. Where you idled, the ghost plays these defaults.
- **The house keeps your excuse** (A). → Shown, not told. First: "His sister is ___" from the letter's three words; next morning she crosses the studio as written.
- **Leave it be, and Roderick tells you one true thing** (M). → Candour is one line, never a menu: "I saw it too" for "Such opinions need no comment" [u26] (s1).
- **The valet ushers** [u07] (s3); his candle (M). → It leads you between rooms, replacing the glove (ART). Below, it leads your ghost, not her.
- **"The tarn would like a word."** (EX). → The only prompt to look down.
- **Below, knock and be explained** (M). → Her map is the Account's back page, *Unaccounted for* (s1): each thing you left be is a door.

## 2. Tensions to resolve
1. **Red:** "what really happened" (old LOOK), "contested" (H, M), "a second hand" (A). → *Red means "not so": someone disagrees.* The hand says who, never who's right. One per screen (ART); rewrite LOOK.md §9.
2. **The Account as writing desk.** → No desk. It writes itself from play; at most one blank an interlude, filled by dragging something found. Roderick reads one line at three suppers, not seven.
3. **The ripple:** swaps (M), rings (H), next moment (A), one step ahead (SP). → *Early*, only.
4. **Roderick lies (H) or omits (M).** → He omits in speech and sings what he omits.
5. **Who cheats in act one?** → Both, with two tells; three edits are hers, chapter I's among them.
6. **Seven act-two turns (M) or four fragments (H, A).** → Seven, uneven: tarn, door, nights 2–3 minutes. Each opens on a reveal and rewrites one Account line.
7. **Idle.** → Never finishes a game. The house leans; the guest writes his default; the ghost plays it.
8. **Mercy rules.** → None. Failure costs something visible, never a retry (H).
9. **Too many secrets.** → One (the shift), two witnesses (physician, Roderick), one twist (the guests). "Madeline is being written" (A) stays mechanism.
10. **"The yet unscrewed lid" [u30]:** in Poe, *yet* means not yet; they screw it down. → Keep the misreading on purpose: a knower's objection names the crime.

## 3. For the first-time player
**Most help:**
- The mirrored P.S. and "The tarn would like a word": looking down, taught in a minute.
- The Nevertheless gag: the tone in 20 seconds.
- One liar per chapter, taught before it's used.
- The flattering reflection, you gallant mid-coffin: funny with no Poe.
- Two of every portrait: a question by minute five.
- Your own recording as opponent: "that's me" needs no reading.
- Consequences shown in the house, not as text (A).

**Needs a way in:**
- Echoes before blows [u40] → the Trist's page turn shows it.
- "We two alone" [u29] → the third bearer, and *Bearers: two*.
- The unscrewed lid → old lids without screws, before the burial.
- *Son cœur* [u01] → glossed in the water: "His heart is a lute; touch it and it sounds."
- The true gust [u49] → show the wind first.
