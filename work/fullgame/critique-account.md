# Critique from the account (02)

## 01 · The House (*The Direct Line*)
- **Strongest idea:** the guest's auto-fill. Leave a blank for ten seconds and he writes it "sensibly and wrongly", so the game literally plays the player and the verb becomes overruling yourself. A close second is the physician as family: his profile in 1775's scrubbed frame turns "the physician of the family" [u07] into a clue.
- **Weakest point, as an evening:** homework. Seven surveys of three or four minutes each; tallies of fourteen and twelve; 1775 and 1809; three hands in the letter. A connoisseur will love the ledger, but a general player goes home saying "I took notes". The mirror act, 16 minutes reached only after an ending, reads as an appendix.
- **Deceit:** mostly fair, often delightful. The missing lace and the wax are tells you can learn, and "Roderick speaks false and sings true" is lovely. But seven liars with two tells each, plus ripple rings whose direction means past or future, is more rule than one evening holds. The rings will feel arbitrary.
- **Build risk:** the art. Six new close-up plates plus `plate.js` is the bottleneck, ahead of the account, the trust system and the mirror mode.

## 03 · The Mirror
- **Strongest idea:** each chapter's turn is played below, so the three acts span the mirror and act two can't become an appendix. Two more are worth stealing: "the lid is never screwed" [u30], which makes your best burial score the crime; and the trap that house and tarn are one witness [u26].
- **Weakest point, as an evening:** you play the same seven games twice. The ghost thrills for two chapters, then it's a rerun. Act one keeps today's short chapters (tarn 5, trist 5, flight 4), so "seven toys" and "plays itself" both survive above. Between chapters, "pin one difference, explain it or leave it" is too thin a verb for 50 minutes.
- **Deceit:** the fairest of the three. "The tarn is true about everyone but whoever is looking" is one rule with many uses. "Some edits were hers" is the most delightful reversal anyone offers: the cheat you caught in chapter one wasn't the house's. The swap mystery may take too much explaining late in act two.
- **Build risk:** seven `mirror` modes amount to seven new games, each needing a recorded ghost good enough to play against. An idle act one makes a weak opponent; their "the house fills the gaps" is itself a second AI.

## Bet
I'd build 03, the mirror: playing against your own recorded hushes is the moment all three of us promise and only it puts at the centre, and its one-day milestone proves that from code we already have. I'd halve its act two and give it the account's page (with 01's auto-fill) as the verb between chapters, because "pin and excuse" can't carry fifty minutes above.
